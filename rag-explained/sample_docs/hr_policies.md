# Acme Corp — HR Policies

## Parental Leave
Acme Corp offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all full-time
employees, regardless of gender, following the birth or adoption of a child.
This is sometimes referred to internally as maternity or paternity leave.
Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival and can be split
into a maximum of two separate blocks.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the following calendar year.
PTO requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the
HR portal.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. Fully remote
arrangements require director-level approval and are reviewed annually.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $100 can be reimbursed without prior approval. Expenses
of $100 or more require manager sign-off before the purchase is made. Submit
receipts within 30 days through the finance portal.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's
note is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.
